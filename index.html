🔍 How We Generate Predictions

Our predictions use statistical analysis of bookmaker odds, team form, and value betting principles. We calculate true probabilities by removing bookmaker margin (vig) from odds, then identify matches where our model's probability exceeds the market price.

Value Bet Formula:
Value = (True Probability × Bookmaker Odds) − 1
We only highlight bets where Value ≥ 0.025 (2.5%+ edge).
